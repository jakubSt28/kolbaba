# The Kolbaba name and logo

Kolbaba's code is free software under the [GNU AGPL v3.0 or later](LICENSE). The name "Kolbaba" and the Kolbaba
logo (the postman's cap, nose and moustache) are not part of that license. They belong to Jakub Šťastný, who
makes Kolbaba, so that people can tell the original from copies.

**You can, without asking:**

- run Kolbaba unchanged, or with your own changes, on your own server for yourself, your family or your company,
  and keep calling it Kolbaba there;
- say that your project is "based on Kolbaba", "a fork of Kolbaba" or "compatible with Kolbaba";
- use the name to write about Kolbaba, review it, or link to it.

**You need your own name and logo when you:**

- publish or distribute a changed version (a fork) for other people;
- offer Kolbaba, changed or not, as a service to people outside your own household or company;
- make anything that could look like it comes from Kolbaba itself.

When in doubt, write to [hello@kolbaba.app](mailto:hello@kolbaba.app).
