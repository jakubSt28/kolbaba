# Code of conduct

Everyone who takes part in Kolbaba (issues, pull requests, discussions, email) is expected to be kind and
respectful, and to assume good intent. We follow the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing other people's private information are not
welcome. Comments, commits or issues that break these rules may be removed, and people who keep breaking them
may be blocked from the project.

To report a problem, email [hello@kolbaba.app](mailto:hello@kolbaba.app). Reports are kept confidential.
